## Login
To login to your live website, type in the URL of your website and add `/admin` at the end of the URL (ex: http://my-website-url.com/admin).


### Environments
Please note that OWOW always delivers two environments: one for staging (testing) and one for live. The credentials to login are identical for both environments, so you can easily log into both.

#### Staging URL
The staging link usually looks like this: `https://develop.your-website.owow.dev/`, where you can also add `/admin` to log in.

#### Live URL
The live link is your actual website URL: `https://your-website.com/admin`

## The CMS
A CMS (Content Management System) is where you manage all content for your website. It allows easy editing of text, images, media, and more without needing to touch the code.

In the left-hand menu of your CMS, you will find various collections, each corresponding to different elements of your website. The layout might differ slightly depending on your website's specific setup.

### Pages
The **Pages** collection allows you to manage the content of all your website's pages. To view all pages, click on **Pages** in the left menu. From there, you can create new pages or edit existing ones.

#### Creating a New Page
To create a new page, click the **Create New** button. You will then be taken to the page creation interface where you can fill out all the necessary fields.

![Create a new page](/manual-images/add-page.png)

- **Title**: The **Page Title** is the name of your page and will be displayed in the browser tab. It’s also the main identifier for the page’s URL, often used as the page slug. You can change the title by editing this field.

---

### Media
The **Media** collection allows you to manage all images, videos, and other media files used across your website. You can upload new files, delete outdated ones, or organize them into folders for better management. Note, this is also possible to do within the block editor.

- Images should have an alt text, and videos should have a caption. This is important for SEO and accessibility. The alt text should be a short description of the image, and the caption should be a longer description of the image.

---

### Optional Collections
This contains collections that are not part of the standard setup but may be added depending on your needs. Examples might include blog posts, events, or testimonials. These are typically flexible collections that allow you to create and organize content that doesn't fall under the main collections like "Pages" or "Media."

---

### Users
The **Users** collection is where you can manage who has access to the CMS and their roles. You can assign different levels of access to different users, such as Admin, Editor, or Viewer.

---

### Social Media
The **Social Media** collection allows you to link your website with social media accounts or manage social sharing settings. 

---

### Redirects
The **Redirects** collection lets you manage URLs that have changed or are outdated. You can create URL redirects to ensure that users and search engines are redirected to the correct pages if URLs are updated.

## Globals
**Globals** are settings that apply across your entire website, such as your website's branding, navigation structure, and integrations.

### Settings
Here you will manage global website settings like timezone, language, and site-wide configurations. These settings often affect things like date formatting, user preferences, or default settings across different pages.

### Brand
The **Brand** global is where you configure your website’s brand identity. This includes setting up your logo, brand colors, fonts, and other visual elements.

### Navigation
The **Navigation** settings allow you to configure the main menu of your website. You can add, remove, or reorder the links that appear in the header or footer of your site. Typically, these links will lead to important pages like "Home," "About Us," "Contact," etc.

### Footer
Here, you can set the content that will appear in your website's footer. This could include your contact details, quick links, social media icons, or legal information.

### Integrations
In this global, you can configure third-party integrations such as analytics, CRM systems, email marketing tools, and more.
